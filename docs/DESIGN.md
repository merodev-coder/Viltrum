## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Core Atmosphere
This design system embodies high-octane biomechanical armor engineering tailored for athletic supremacy and superhero physique culture. Drawing raw kinetic force from comic lore, high-performance compression wear, and cybernetic aerospace tech, the aesthetic is aggressive, elite, disciplined, and unbreakable. 

### Target Audience
Athletes, bodybuilders, combat sport practitioners, and pop-culture enthusiasts who view their fitness regimen as battle conditioning and their activewear as forged ballistic armor.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Tenets
The design movement balances **Tactical Cyber-Brutalism** with **Hyper-Precision Luxury Dark Mode**:
- **Monolithic Surfaces:** Deep void black and obsidian bases with sharp metallic dividing lines rather than soft bubbly containers.
- **Biomechanical Kinetic Accents:** Incisive crimson energy highlights, radioactive amber indicators, and high-frequency UI data trackers.
- **Industrial Precision:** HUD-inspired telemetry labels, mono-spaced specification metadata, and zero-compromise mechanical contrast.

## Colors

The palette leverages a calibrated high-contrast hierarchy designed specifically for deep OLED screens and nocturnal high-focus shopping experiences.

### Key Roles
- **Primary (`#E61938` / `crimson-glow`):** The kinetic power core. Used for decisive call-to-actions, bundle progress completion bars, primary active states, and tactical pricing highlights.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B` / `amber-warning`):** Thermal alert status. Used for special bundle pricing tags, verified buyer badges, armory warnings, and star review highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#38BDF8` / `electric-cyan`):** Cyber telemetry. Reserved for high-tech technical specs, breathable fabric stats, and compression ratings.
- **Neutral (`#08090C` / `void-black`):** Substrate foundation. An unforgiving near-absolute black infused with 1% deep cold slate to eliminate optical banding while maximizing graphic punch.

### Tonal Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base:** `#040406` (Void Black)
- **Layer 1 Surface (Cards / Bins):** `#111318` (Armor Slate)
- **Layer 2 Surface (Interactive Slits / Hover Slots):** `#181B22` (Armor Plate)
- **Structural Outlines:** `#282D37` (Cyber Border)
- **Foreground Primary Text:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White, high impact)
- **Foreground Secondary Text:** `#94A3B8` (Military Silver / Cool Gray)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy creates visceral athletic momentum. 

- **Display & Headlines (`Anton`):** Heavyweight, condensed, athletic, commanding. All primary brand announcements, hero battle slogans, and armory module titles must be rendered in ALL CAPS to channel uncompromising military-grade authority.
- **Narrative & Body (`Space Grotesk`):** Clean, geometric, sharp, modern. Balances readability for multi-line hero descriptions and sizing parameters while retaining a scientific, mechanical edge.
- **Technical & UI Telemetry (`JetBrains Mono`):** Monospace precision. Dedicated to micro-badges, stock status, armor composition metrics, pricing readouts, and currency identifiers (`EGP`). Always styled with increased letter-spacing.

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid Philosophy
The viewport utilizes a tactical fluid grid governed by rigid horizontal cutlines and dense structural alignment. 

- **Breakpoints:**
  - `Desktop (>= 1280px)`: 12-column layout with 24px gutters, max-width bounded container (`1440px`), outer margin `48px`.
  - `Tablet (768px - 1279px)`: 8-column layout with 16px gutters, outer margin `24px`.
  - `Mobile (< 768px)`: 4-column layout with 12px gutters, outer margin `16px`.

### Spacing Rhythm
Vertical spacing between discrete layout zones is exaggerated to produce cinematic pauses:
- **Hero & Module Divisors:** `space-xl` (48px - 96px on desktop) partitioned with hairline metallic dividers.
- **Card Internals:** Strict `space-sm` (8px) to `space-md` (16px) packing to echo high-density ballistic loadouts.
- **Tactical Micro-spacing:** Badges, price-slits, and metadata use `space-xs` (4px) margins.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on soft diffused drop-shadows. Instead, elevation is expressed through **Chiseled Tonal Layering**, **Cyber-Metallic Hairlines**, and **Direct Backlit Luminescence**.

### Depth Layers
- **Sub-Floor (`#040406`):** The infinite dark backdrop with subtle 40px linear cybernetic circuit grid lines (1.5% opacity).
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#111318`):** Product cards, carousel tiles, and bundle builder dock. Wrapped in a 1px solid border (`#282D37`).
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#181B22`):** Dropdowns, active slot targets, hover states. Outlined with 1px border (`#3F4654`).
- **Surface Elevation 3 (Floating HUD / Modals):** Translucent armor plate (`#0C0E13` at 92% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) with crisp crimson or amber apex corner borders.

### Luminescence & Energy Signatures
- **Active State / Primary CTA:** High-voltage edge glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(230, 25, 56, 0.45)`.
- **Special Tier Highlight:** Amber radiation: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a low-radius, architectural profile reflecting forged plates and carbon fiber cuts:

- **Base Radius (`roundedness: 1`):** Default `4px` corner radius across cards, inputs, and modular slots. This prevents the interface from feeling brittle while sustaining an aggressive military posture.
- **Chiseled Chamfer Accent:** Tactical chips, discount tags, and primary CTAs may utilize a 45-degree corner clip-path (`clip-path: polygon(0 0, calc(100% - 8px) 0, 100% 8px, 100% 100%, 0 100%)`) on top-right edges for distinct tech-armor styling.
- **Divider Cuts:** Thin 1px gradient dividers transitioning from transparent to `#282D37` to transparent.

## Components

### 1. Tactical Action Buttons
- **Primary Combat CTA ("BUILD YOUR BUNDLE" / "ORDER SUIT"):**
  - Background: Solid Electric Crimson (`#E61938`) with 45° chamfered top-right corner.
  - Typography: `label-btn` in pure white with ALL CAPS tracking (`0.06em`).
  - Micro-details: Left-side 2px wide vertical white energy strip; on hover, shifts background to `#FF2A4D` with crimson drop glow and 2px rightward arrow kick.
- **Secondary Ghost Action ("ENTER THE ARCHIVE"):**
  - Background: Transparent; Border: 1px solid `#3F4654`.
  - Typography: `label-btn` in `#FFFFFF`. Hover triggers `#181B22` background fill and white border highlight.

### 2. Armor Product Cards
- **Container:** Dark charcoal slate (`#111318`), 1px solid border (`#282D37`), `roundedness: 1` (4px).
- **Product Display Frame:** Contrast gradient spotlight background (`#161A22` to `#0B0C0F`) isolating the compression garment render.
- **Badges:** Top-left tech chip (`HERO EDITION`) with `tech-caption` typography, dark background, and 1px border. Top-right discount badge in high-intensity crimson (`-20%`).
- **Data Strip:** Product name in `headline-md`, active price in pure white (`EGP 480`) accompanied by crossed-out base price in muted gray (`EGP 600`).
- **Hover Micro-interaction:** 1px border glows crimson (`#E61938`), product image scales up by 4% with smooth cubic-bezier easing.

### 3. Interactive Bundle Builder Dock
- **Architecture:** Dual-bay weapon loadout HUD layout.
- **Slot Bins:** Dotted metallic perimeter (`border: 1px dashed #3F4654`) with interactive plus icon when unselected. Once loaded with armor piece, transitions to a solid plate with neon green or red completion tick.
- **Live Armor Calculator:** Persistent floating bottom HUD bar displaying dynamic savings calculations (`SAVE 110 EGP`) highlighted in amber warning text (`#F59E0B`), bundle price in bold white typography, and high-impact CTA button.

### 4. Customer Review Armor Feed
- **Card Architecture:** Precision quote containers with 5-star tactical gold indicators (`#F59E0B`).
- **Telemetry Stamp:** Verified warrior badge, item purchased model tag rendered in `tech-data` JetBrains Mono, and quote in `body-md` Space Grotesk.
- **Carousel Controls:** Angular segmented pagination pills (Active pill stretched into crimson bar, inactive pills as gray dots).

### 5. Tactical Navigation Bar
- **Surface:** Pitch black glass (`rgba(4, 4, 6, 0.85)` with 20px blur), locked to screen top with a continuous 1px bottom border (`#181B22`).
- **Brand Mark:** Viltrum seal in high-polish chrome finish alongside condensed display typography.
- **Links:** All caps, hover states accented with an animated 2px bottom crimson underscore indicator.
- **Cart / Armor Cache Drawer:** Compact HUD counter badge with active crimson count ring.

### 6. Inputs & Selectors
- **Fields (Size Selector / Search):** Solid `#0E1015` fill, 1px border `#282D37`, focus state turns border to `#E61938` with zero border radius rounding beyond 4px. Placeholder text rendered in muted steel (`#64748B`).
